Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. For flood emergencies anywhere in Trenton, Trenton Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. Our technicians rely on psychrometric calculations, meter readings and thermal imaging to verify that each affected spot, from the framing to the subfloor to the inside of the walls, meets accepted drying standards. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation

Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • Rainwater getting inside through a roof or wall damaged by a storm
